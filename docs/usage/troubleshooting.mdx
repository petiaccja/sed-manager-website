# Troubleshooting

## Stack reset

Sometimes, the device's protocol stack gets desynchronized from SEDManager's, which results in broken communication between the two. The issue will manifest itself unusual error messages such as *security provider busy* or *NVMe protocol violation*, or timeouts. This can happen if you forcefully terminate SEDManager in the middle of a session, your operating system crashes, or due to a bug in SEDManager or the device.

When requesting a stack reset, the device resets its protocol stack to its starting state, where SEDManager can reestablish communication, effectively fixing the issue.

## Non-compliant drives

Some devices don't implement the TCG specification properly, and may refuse to respond to SEDManager's communication requests at all. In these cases, you will typically see a timeout or an unexpected error at another phase. Unfortunately, stack reset cannot help with non-compliant drives.
