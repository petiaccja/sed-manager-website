# Taking ownership

## Setting the owner's password

The [owner authority](/docs/theory/the_tcg_spec#the-opal-opalite-pyrite-and-ruby-sscs) comes with a factory-configured default password. In order to prevent others from modifying the device's configuration, that password needs to be changed, which is what we mean by *taking ownership*.

As the owner of the drive, you, and only you, can:
- Activate the locking features of the drive
- Revert the locking features to factory defaults and erase all data in the meantime
- Revert the entire device to factory defaults

While encrypted data is not accessible even for the owner, other people won't be able to perform any of the above.

:::info Reverting the device when you lost your password

Aside from the owner, there is the so-called *physical owner*, whose password is typically printed on the device under the *PSID* label. With the physical owner's password, you can factory reset your device even when you locked yourself out as the owner.

:::

:::warning You may brick your device if you lose your passwords

Losing access both the owner and physical owner password means you don't have any way to factory reset the device. While you can still use the drive if you have your passwords to access the data, losing that as well means your drive is unusable and you need to buy a new one.

:::
